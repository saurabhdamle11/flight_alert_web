About

Get pinged when a plane flies over you.

What it does

Flight Whisperer sends a notification to your phone or computer when an aircraft flies within about 25 km of a location you choose. There's no app to download and no account to create — it runs in your browser.

How it works

  • Choose a location. Use your device's location or search for a city.
  • Turn on alerts. Your browser asks for permission to show notifications.
  • Get pinged. When a plane passes overhead, you get a notification. Tap it to see the flight on Flightradar24.
  • Turn off any time. Unsubscribing stops alerts and deletes your saved location.

iPhone and iPad

On iOS 16.4 or later, notifications only work after you add Flight Whisperer to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.
