Privacy policy

The short version

Flight Whisperer only keeps what it needs to tell you when a plane is overhead: an approximate location and a way to send your browser a notification. There are no accounts, names or email addresses. Unsubscribing deletes it all.

What we store

  • Your location, rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places), plus a label such as "Current location" or the city you picked. Used only to match aircraft passing near you.
  • A push subscription: the address your browser provides so we can send it notifications. It doesn't identify you personally.
  • A random device token, kept in your browser's local storage, so this device can manage its own alerts. It isn't a cookie and isn't used for tracking.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo receives the text you type in the city search, to look up places. Using your device's location instead skips this.
  • Your browser's push service (run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser) delivers notifications to your device.
  • OpenSky Network supplies flight positions. Your data isn't sent to them.
  • Flightradar24 opens when you tap a notification, and their own privacy policy applies there.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and provides privacy-friendly Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

Deleting your data

Press Turn off & delete my data on the main page. This removes your push subscription and deletes your saved location and device token from our server straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
